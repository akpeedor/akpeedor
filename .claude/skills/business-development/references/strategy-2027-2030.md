# Strategy 2027–2030

Agreed: 2026-11-06. Horizon: 3 years (2027 → 2030).

## Core principle

One founder, one revenue source today. The biggest risk is spreading time and cash across four things before one can fund the others. **RND pays for everything; every new venture proves itself before it gets more money or time.**

## The money rule

Split every naira RND earns as soon as it arrives. Starting percentages — adjust once real figures are in `numbers.md`:

| Bucket | Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| RND operating costs | 50% | Running the business |
| Founder salary | 15–20% | Paid on purpose, not from leftovers |
| Tax reserve | 10% | |
| Emergency reserve | 10% | Until it covers 6 months of costs |
| Venture fund | 10% | The **only** money Oneal and 37 Signals can use |

If a venture runs out of its share, it waits. It never borrows from RND's operating money.

## Roadmap

### 2027 — Cash and proof
- RND: stable monthly profit; raise prices; add retainer clients; reduce day-to-day dependence on the founder.
- Pan Oneal Global: first lean pilot cohort (5–10 clinics/schools) using partners' money and space.
- 37 Signals & Co: finance services win first paying clients. **No app.**
- Symmetry Group: brand identity; settle the 37 Signals name.

### 2028 — Partners and structure
- Bring in co-founders (fintech technical lead, compliance advisor).
- Launch first non-custodial trading tool.
- Formalize Symmetry Group as a holding company if the numbers support it.

### 2029–2030 — Scale
- Raise outside money for whichever venture has proven traction.
- Begin a licensed trading product via a regulated partner or an acquired licence.

## Co-founder and partner rules

- Good people join a clear plan or proof, not a vision alone. Recruit after there's something to show.
- Equity per company, never at Symmetry Group level.
- 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff for everyone, friends included.
- Advisors: 0.5–1% advisory equity, with defined deliverables.
- 37 Signals recruiting starts Q3–Q4 2027, after finance services have clients.
