## 37 Signals & Co detail

Founder is an active, profitable forex trader (paused trading to focus on RND). 37 Signals & Co is Nigerian-based; a Delaware entity is not affordable now.

Three layers, in order:
1. **Financial media and research hub** — financial news, market reports, newsletter, big buys/sells, M&A, US/UK market and trading-firm analysis. Feasible now. RND can produce the content.
2. **Founder's own trading with own capital** — build a verified, public track record (e.g. Myfxbook / FX Blue). This track record is the asset that makes everything later credible.
3. **Copy trading / strategy app** — later. SEC Nigeria's draft Rules on Online Forex & CFD (published 1 Sept 2026, not yet in force) license brokers, introducing brokers and technology/platform providers (reported ₦3bn–₦5bn capital), define a regulated "Copy Manager (Strategy Provider)", reach offshore platforms targeting Nigerians, and ban unapproved influencers. Realistic path: become a strategy provider on an already licensed platform once rules are final, not build our own platform.
- Do **not** start a retail prop firm: revenue mostly comes from challenge fees paid by traders who fail; regulatory grey area in Nigeria; reputational risk.

**Financial services arm (A)** — financial modeling, analysis, accounting, business development.
- Feasible now: low capital, service income.
- First customers: RND and Oneal clients, Oneal cohort startups.

**Trading app (B) — original concept, now layer 3 above** — built on third-party APIs (broker/partner handles custody and execution).
- Not feasible as a full product in 2027.
- User does not want to launch fully in Nigeria because of licensing costs. Note: launching elsewhere still means that country's rules apply; partners will check compliance; Nigeria's Investments and Securities Act 2025 widened SEC scope (including digital assets).
- Path: start with non-custodial tools (trading journal, analytics, portfolio tracker, education). "Signals" may count as regulated investment advice → get a legal opinion first.
- Comparable Nigerian players: Bamboo, Risevest, Chaka (used US broker APIs such as DriveWealth).

**Open issue — name:** "37signals" is a well-known US software company (Basecamp, HEY). Registering in Nigeria avoids a US company-name clash, but online (domain, social handles, search, app stores) the names collide globally. Check CAC name availability and trademark search before branding spend.

**Open issue — tax:** professional services (e.g. accounting, advisory) are excluded from the small-company tax exemption under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, so the finance-services arm may pay CIT regardless of size. Confirm with a tax adviser.
