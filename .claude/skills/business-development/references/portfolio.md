## 37 Signals & Co detail

**A. Financial services firm** — financial modeling, analysis, accounting, business development.
- Feasible now: low capital, service income.
- First customers: RND and Oneal clients, Oneal cohort startups.

**B. Trading app** — built on third-party APIs (broker/partner handles custody and execution).
- Not feasible as a full product in 2027.
- User does not want to launch fully in Nigeria because of licensing costs. Note: launching elsewhere still means that country's rules apply; partners will check compliance; Nigeria's Investments and Securities Act 2025 widened SEC scope (including digital assets).
- Path: start with non-custodial tools (trading journal, analytics, portfolio tracker, education). "Signals" may count as regulated investment advice → get a legal opinion first.
- Comparable Nigerian players: Bamboo, Risevest, Chaka (used US broker APIs such as DriveWealth).

**Open issue — name:** "37signals" is a well-known US software company (Basecamp, HEY). Trademark and search risk. Decide on the final name before spending on branding.
